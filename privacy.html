DubLite Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

The short version

DubLite does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no ads.

What stays on your phone

The app counts how many minutes you spend in each site today and shows it on the home screen. That number is saved only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Instagram and YouTube

DubLite opens the regular Instagram and YouTube websites inside the app and hides parts of them, such as Reels and Shorts. When you log in, you log in directly on Instagram's or Google's own page. DubLite never sees or stores your password, messages, or anything else you do on those sites. Your use of those sites is covered by their own privacy policies.

Not affiliated

DubLite is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram, Meta, YouTube, or Google. Those names are trademarks of their owners and are used only to say which websites DubLite works with.

Blocking rules

When the app starts, it downloads a small file listing which parts of each site to hide, from GitHub Pages. That request contains no information about you, but GitHub may log the IP address that made it, as with any website.

Children

DubLite collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact
